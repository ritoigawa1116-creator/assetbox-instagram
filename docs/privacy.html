1. 本アプリの目的

本アプリは、当法人が自ら管理する Instagram ビジネスアカウントのためだけに使用する社内用ツールです。第三者に提供・公開するサービスではありません。主な機能は次のとおりです。

2. 取得する情報

  • 当法人のアカウントの投稿に寄せられたコメント（投稿者のユーザーネーム、本文、日時）
  • 当法人のアカウント宛てに送られたダイレクトメッセージ（送信者のユーザーネーム、本文、日時）
  • 当法人のアカウントのフォロワー数・投稿数

上記以外の情報（他のアカウントの投稿、フォロワーの一覧、位置情報、連絡先など）は取得しません。

3. 利用目的

  • お問い合わせ・ご相談への返信漏れを防ぎ、適切に対応するため
  • ご相談の内容を整理し、空き家・相続不動産・山林等に関する相談対応の改善に役立てるため
  • 個人を特定しない形での件数・傾向の集計（社内報告）のため

Privacy Policy (English)

Asset Box (General Incorporated Association) — Effective October 28, 2026

1. Purpose of the app

"AssetBox Poster" is an internal tool used only by Asset Box to manage its own Instagram business account (@assetbox_3314). It is not offered to the public. It is used to (a) publish scheduled posts to our own account, (b) read comments and direct messages sent to our own account so that staff are notified and no inquiry goes unanswered, and (c) compile counts (inquiries, followers) for internal reports.

2. Data we access

Comments on our account's posts and direct messages sent to our account (sender's username, text, timestamp), and our account's follower and media counts. We do not access any other data.

3. How we use it

Only to respond to inquiries, to improve our consultation service, and to produce anonymized statistics for internal reporting. We never sell the data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as required by law or with the person's prior consent.

4. Storage

Data is stored in an access-restricted Google Sheets file viewable only by our representative and staff who handle inquiries. Records are kept for about three years after the inquiry is resolved and then deleted.

5. Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, send "Data deletion request" via Instagram DM to @assetbox_3314, or email office@asset-box.jp with your Instagram username. We will delete your records within 30 days and confirm.
